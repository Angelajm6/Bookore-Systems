# Bookore Systems — construction outreach starter

## Best first audience

Owner-led residential remodelers, GCs, design-build firms, and specialty contractors with enough project value that a slow lead response, unworked estimate, or weak handoff is expensive. Start with one niche and geography when prospecting.

Lead with one operational leak, not “AI automation.”

| Offer | Best signal | Opening question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead-to-Site-Visit | New lead channel, growing service area, sales hiring | “When a good project inquiry comes in, how reliably does it reach a site visit?” |
| Estimate Follow-Up | Active quoting, estimating hire, visible project pipeline | “What ensures every active estimate gets a useful next touch before it goes stale?” |
| Project Handoff | PM hiring, multiple crews, recent growth | “How does a sold job move from sales to production without someone reconstructing the scope?” |

## Personalize with

- One true public signal about the company or role.
- One workflow likely to be costly or fragile for them.
- One concrete outcome: faster site-visit booking, a decision on an estimate, or a cleaner sold-job handoff.

Avoid claiming results you cannot yet support with client evidence.
