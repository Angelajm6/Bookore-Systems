# Bookore Systems — outreach starter

## Best first audience

Growing B2B SaaS and service businesses with 10–100 employees. Look for a visible signal: hiring in customer success or operations, a new service launch, a founder still handling sales follow-up, or a busy calendar of demos and client calls.

Lead with one operational problem—not “AI automation.”

## Three angles to test

| System | Best signal | Opening question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead Response Engine | Demos, inbound demand, sales hiring | “How quickly does a good inbound lead get a useful response today?” |
| Client Onboarding Hub | New clients, implementation team, service launch | “What has to happen between ‘closed won’ and a confident kickoff?” |
| Meeting-to-Momentum | Customer-success or agency teams, frequent calls | “Where do the decisions from client calls live once the meeting ends?” |

## What to personalize

- One true, public signal about the company or the person’s role.
- The single workflow most likely to be expensive or fragile for them.
- A concrete, human outcome: a faster response, a prepared kickoff, or a clear recap.

Avoid claiming time savings or results before you have evidence from a client engagement.
